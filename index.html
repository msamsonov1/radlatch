Substrate Microphysics & Dynamic Inversion Theory

In advanced deep-submicron CMOS flight processors (e.g., 7 nm / 16 nm FinFET commercial off-the-shelf system-on-chips), ion tracks from heavy galactic cosmic rays deposit ionized charge columns. This triggers regenerative four-layer $p\text{-}n\text{-}p\text{-}n$ silicon-controlled rectifier (SCR) latch-up states. RadLatch™ replaces slow current sense resistors with an ultrafast dual-variable $(D, Z)$ state observer executing in nanoseconds on a co-packaged GaN/GaAs power plane.

Physics Pillar 01 Micro-Plasma Kinetics

Heavy-Ion Charge Deposition & Regeneration

When a high-LET particle (e.g., $^{56}\text{Fe}$ at $\text{LET} \ge 37\,\text{MeV}\cdot\text{cm}^2/\text{mg}$) traverses the well-substrate boundary, it generates a dense electron-hole plasma cloud. The lateral substrate displacement current turns on the parasitic vertical NPN and lateral PNP bipolar junctions:

$$Q_{\text{dep}} = \int_0^L \frac{dE}{dx}(x) \cdot \frac{\rho_{\text{Si}}}{W_{\text{ehp}}}\,dx \implies \beta_{\text{NPN}} \cdot \beta_{\text{PNP}} \ge 1.0$$

Once the product of the cross-coupled bipolar gains satisfies $\beta_1 \beta_2 \ge 1$, regenerative feedback latches the device into a low-impedance holding state with holding current $I_h \gg I_{\text{nominal}}$.

Physics Pillar 02 Power Delivery Network

The Regulator Saturation Paradox

Standard point-of-load (POL) synchronous buck converters rely on voltage-mode or average-current feedback. When an SEL collapses rail impedance $Z_{\text{rail}} \to 0$, the error amplifier perceives a voltage droop and drives pulse-width modulation (PWM) duty cycle $D$ to its maximum ceiling $D_{\text{sat}}$:

$$V_{\text{out}}(t) = D(t) \cdot V_{\text{in}} - L \frac{dI_L}{dt} - I_L(R_{\text{FET}} + Z_{\text{rail}})$$

As $Z_{\text{rail}} \to 0$, the controller drives $D \to 1.0$ (saturation), pumping maximum energy into the silicon junction and accelerating catastrophic metal line electromigration and gate oxide breakdown.

Buck Switching Freq: $f_{sw} = 2.4\,\text{MHz}$ $D_{\text{sat}} \ge 98.5\%$
Physics Pillar 03 State Observer Algorithm

Phase-Space Inversion & Cleansing Trigger

RadLatch continuously samples the high-frequency rail vector $[V_{\text{rail}}, I_{\text{rail}}, D]$ at $200\,\text{MSPS}$. Dynamic impedance is computed in hardware via synthetic derivative tracking:

$$\mathcal{S}_{\text{SEL}}(t) = \Theta\left(\frac{d D}{dt} - \kappa_D\right) \cdot \Theta\left(Z_0 - Z_{\text{rail}}(t)\right) \cdot \left[\frac{D(t)}{Z_{\text{rail}}(t)}\right] > \Gamma_{\text{trip}}$$

If state metric $\mathcal{S}_{\text{SEL}}$ crosses trigger threshold $\Gamma_{\text{trip}}$, the system discriminates a genuine SEL from benign CPU load steps (which cause transient droops but never sustained $Z \to 0$ with $D \to 1$).

Sampling ADC: 12-Bit 250 MSPS Discrimination: < 2.6 ns
Physics Pillar 04 Hardware Substrate

E-Mode GaN Crowbar & Charge Depletion

Upon tripping $\mathcal{S}_{\text{SEL}}$, a monolithic Enhancement-Mode Gallium Nitride (e-GaN) high-side switch opens in $< 1.8\,\text{ns}$ while a low-side shunt rapidly dumps decoupling rail capacitance:

$$t_{\text{clear}} = \tau_{\text{quench}} \ge 3 \cdot \tau_{\text{recomb}} = 3 \left(\frac{1}{\sigma v_{\text{th}} N_t} + \tau_{\text{Auger}}\right) \approx 45 \text{ to } 90\,\mu\text{s}$$

Starving the parasitic thyristor below holding current $I_h$ for duration $t > \tau_{\text{quench}}$ forces complete carrier recombination. Power is ramped back up cleanly without corrupting static SRAM register states.
